## Important notes for production
1. **Leo and the bangle woman are persona stories**, not documented facts. This follows the channel's existing style (e.g. "the woman with the 1968 ring" in the $3 Liquid video). The historical context around them (the Shanghai refugees, B jade in the 1980s) is real. The timeline is internally consistent: a bangle bought in 1991 can be B jade, which only appeared in the 1980s.
2. The script **never claims** that the black light proves jade is real. It says "a strong glow is a no, a dark stone is a maybe, it is never a yes." This protects the channel from angry comments and matches what GIA says.
3. The script deliberately leaves out unsafe tests from other videos (the lighter/hair trick, a knife on the face of a carving, acids). It warns against them instead.
4. Facts I **did not include** because I could not confirm them: "jadeite 24× harder to break than a diamond" (Jewels of the Trade), and "May 2026 Christie's $25.5M jadeite necklace" (Natalie Pencer, unconfirmed).
