Do you really need a rooster for your hens to lay eggs? 🐔 No, and that's only the first of 8 backyard chicken myths beginners hear all the time.

In this video, Martha Bennett busts 8 of the most common backyard chicken myths using real research from university extension services, Purina, and peer-reviewed science, so you can stop guessing and care for your flock with confidence.

🥚 In this video, you'll learn:

1. Do hens need a rooster to lay eggs? How a hen makes an egg in about 25 hours, and why fertile eggs aren't more nutritious
2. Do hens stop laying after one year? Realistic egg production by age, and where the one-year myth really comes from
3. Should a good hen lay every day? Why daylight matters more than any feed or supplement
4. Do chickens need a heat lamp in winter? Why moisture, not cold, is the real danger, and why heat lamps cause barn fires
5. Is a small coop fine? How much space your hens actually need
6. Do you have to clean the coop every day? How the deep litter method works
7. Will chickens keep your yard pest-free? What the research says about chickens and ticks
8. Are chickens stupid? The surprising science of chicken intelligence, and where "pecking order" comes from
